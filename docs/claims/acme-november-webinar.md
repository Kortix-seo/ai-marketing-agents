## How the rows are used

The first two rows come from the pricing page. A reader can open the URL and check each sentence against the figures and the wording on it.

The third row points at the team's own events page, which is why the placeholder domain `acme.example` stands in for it. Swap in your own URL. On the Monday the page stops listing 4 November, the claim becomes `[STALE: the Acme events page no longer lists 4 November]` and the read date stays at 2026-09-08 until a person writes the new line and dates it.

The fourth row records a sentence with no page behind it. The draft carries `[NO SOURCE]` in that position, and the row stays in this list until somebody finds the page or drops the sentence. Deleting the row is how a gap gets forgotten.

A row is edited only when its date is edited with it. A row is deleted when its sentence leaves the draft.
